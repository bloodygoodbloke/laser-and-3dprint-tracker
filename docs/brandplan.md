MakerFlow Brand Kit
1. Logo Concept — Flow Path M Monogram
Core Idea
A single continuous line forms a stylised M, symbolising:
• smooth workflow
• connected processes
• maker‑friendly simplicity
• modern SaaS identity
Shape Breakdown
• One unbroken stroke
• Rounded corners for friendliness
• Slight upward curves to imply motion
• Balanced symmetry for a clean, professional look
Visual Style
• Minimalist
• Geometric
• Works at tiny sizes (app icon)
• Works in vinyl, laser engraving, and print
Suggested Variants
• Primary: Flow Path M + “MakerFlow” wordmark
• Compact: Flow Path M only
• Badge: Flow Path M inside a circle
• Mono: Single‑colour outline for engraving
---
2. Colour Palette
Default app background
• White — #FFFFFF

Brand blues
• Primary Blue — #3A7DFF (primary actions and selected navigation)
• Dark Blue — #1D63E7 (hover and pressed states)
• Light Blue — #5DA8FF (highlights and supporting accents)

Neutrals
• Charcoal — #1C1C1E (primary text and structure)
• Soft Grey — #E5E5E7 (inputs, borders, and neutral surfaces)

Usage Guidelines
• Use the white background and charcoal text for the default light theme.
• Use Primary Blue for main actions and highlights; use Dark Blue for hover states.
• Keep Light Blue for supporting highlights so it does not compete with primary actions.
• Use Soft Grey for inputs and neutral areas.
---
3. Typography
Primary Font
Inter — clean, modern, highly readable.
Fallbacks
System UI, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif.
---
4. Brand Voice & Messaging
Tone
• Friendly but professional
• Maker‑centric
• Clear, practical
• “Built by a maker, for makers”
Messaging Pillars
• Organisation — “Your workshop, organised.”
• Efficiency — “Track jobs, materials, and customers effortlessly.”
• Clarity — “Know exactly what’s happening in your workshop.”
• Growth — “Turn your hobby or shop into a streamlined business.”
Words to Use
Flow, track, organise, jobs, materials, workshop, maker
Words to Avoid
Corporate jargon, overly technical manufacturing terms
---
5. Logo Usage Rules
Clear Space
Minimum: 1× the height of the M around the logo.
Minimum Size
• App icon: 32×32 px
• Print: 10 mm height
Backgrounds
• Best on white, charcoal, or soft grey
• Avoid busy textures
Colour Rules
• Primary: Electric Blue
• Secondary: Charcoal
• Mono: Black or white
---
6. Iconography Style
General Style
• Rounded corners
• Line‑based icons
• 2.5 px stroke weight
• Minimal detail
Maker Icons
Laser beam, 3D printer nozzle, material sheet, gear, flow arrows
---
7. UI Style Guide
Buttons
• Primary Blue for primary actions
• Dark Blue for hover and pressed states
• Use a readable label color with strong contrast
• Keep existing rounded button shapes consistent
Cards & Panels
• White or Soft Grey surfaces
• Charcoal text
• Subtle borders and shadows
Navigation
• Light background with charcoal labels
• Primary Blue for selected states and key highlights
