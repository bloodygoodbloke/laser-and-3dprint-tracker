MakerFlow Brand Kit
1. Logo Concept — Flow Path M Monogram
Core Idea
A single continuous line forms a stylised M, symbolising:
• smooth workflow
• connected processes
• maker‑friendly simplicity
• modern SaaS identity
Shape Breakdown
• One unbroken stroke
• Rounded corners for friendliness
• Slight upward curves to imply motion
• Balanced symmetry for a clean, professional look
Visual Style
• Minimalist
• Geometric
• Works at tiny sizes (app icon)
• Works in vinyl, laser engraving, and print
Suggested Variants
• Primary: Flow Path M + “MakerFlow” wordmark
• Compact: Flow Path M only
• Badge: Flow Path M inside a circle
• Mono: Single‑colour outline for engraving
---
2. Colour Palette
Primary Colours
• Electric Blue — #3A7DFF
• Charcoal Black — #1C1C1E
Accent Colours
• Warm Orange — #FF8A3A
• Soft Grey — #E5E5E7
Usage Guidelines
• Blue for primary actions, highlights, and logo
• Charcoal for text and UI structure
• Orange for callouts or maker‑energy accents
• Soft Grey for panels, cards, and neutral areas
---
3. Typography
Primary Font
Inter — clean, modern, highly readable
Secondary Font
Montserrat — ideal for headings and marketing
Fallbacks
Arial, Helvetica, system UI fonts
---
4. Brand Voice & Messaging
Tone
• Friendly but professional
• Maker‑centric
• Clear, practical
• “Built by a maker, for makers”
Messaging Pillars
• Organisation — “Your workshop, organised.”
• Efficiency — “Track jobs, materials, and customers effortlessly.”
• Clarity — “Know exactly what’s happening in your workshop.”
• Growth — “Turn your hobby or shop into a streamlined business.”
Words to Use
Flow, track, organise, jobs, materials, workshop, maker
Words to Avoid
Corporate jargon, overly technical manufacturing terms
---
5. Logo Usage Rules
Clear Space
Minimum: 1× the height of the M around the logo.
Minimum Size
• App icon: 32×32 px
• Print: 10 mm height
Backgrounds
• Best on white, charcoal, or soft grey
• Avoid busy textures
Colour Rules
• Primary: Electric Blue
• Secondary: Charcoal
• Mono: Black or white
---
6. Iconography Style
General Style
• Rounded corners
• Line‑based icons
• 2.5 px stroke weight
• Minimal detail
Maker Icons
Laser beam, 3D printer nozzle, material sheet, gear, flow arrows
---

Cards & Panels
